Operational Matrix

Full-Spectrum Security & Logistics

Security Division

JPT LLC executes high-level threat mitigation and executive protection. Our methodology relies on proactive intelligence rather than reactive force.

Advance Intelligence & Sweeps

Before our principals arrive, JPT advance teams scout locations, identify choke points, locate the nearest trauma centers, and execute counter-surveillance measures to ensure the operating theater is secure.

Armed Close Protection

Deployment of highly trained, CCW-certified operators. Our agents maintain a low-profile footprint while providing an impenetrable physical perimeter for executives, politicians, and high-net-worth individuals.

Event & Summit Security

Large-scale perimeter control, credential management, and crowd mitigation for corporate summits, private events, and VIP gatherings. We integrate seamlessly with local and federal law enforcement.

High-Threat Extraction

Rapid response coordination for volatile environments. Our teams are trained in tactical driving and evasive maneuvering to secure and extract principals from compromised locations safely.

Transportation Division

The logistical arm of JPT LLC ensures that the movement of our principals and their teams is as secure and efficient as the boots on the ground.

Secure Tarmac Access

Direct to jet boarding capabilities at major FBOs across Southern California, including LAX, VNY, and BUR. We eliminate public terminal exposure entirely.

Motorcoach Logistics

Large scale transportation for professional and collegiate athletic rosters. Our 56-passenger motorcoaches feature extensive undercoach storage for equipment and integrate with police escorts for rapid stadium deployment.

Armored Fleet Deployment

Available upon advanced request, JPT can deploy fully up-armored vehicles driven by tactical agents to mitigate ballistic threats in elevated risk scenarios.

Convoy Management

Coordinated movement of multiple vehicles, integrating lead and follow cars, encrypted comms, and active route monitoring to ensure safe transit for corporate tours and VIP entourages.
